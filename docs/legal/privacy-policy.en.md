# Woni Privacy Policy

Jungkuk Lee ("Developer") provides the personal budgeting service "Woni" ("Service"). The Developer complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea and other applicable laws, and establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy to protect users' personal information and to handle related concerns promptly.

## 1. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Developer processes personal information for the following purposes only, and does not use it for any other purpose.

1. Member identification and social login authentication
2. Server synchronization of budgeting data entered by the user, and restoration when changing devices
3. Responding to and verifying user inquiries
4. Preventing misuse of the Service and maintaining stable operation

## 2. Categories of Personal Information Processed

### a. Information collected at sign-up and login (social login)

| Login method | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Google | Email address, name, profile image URL, Google account identifier |
| Apple | Email address, name (only where the user consents to provide it), Apple account identifier |
| Automatically generated | Member identifier (UUID), sign-up date and time, last login date and time |

- If you use Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, the Developer collects the anonymous relay address provided by Apple instead of your actual email address.
- The Developer does not receive or store passwords from social login providers.

### b. Information entered directly by the user while using the Service

Budgeting data: income/expense type, amount, currency, category, asset, transaction date, memo, and applied exchange rate.

### c. Non-members (users who do not log in)

The Developer collects no personal information from Non-members. Budgeting data entered by a Non-member is not transmitted to the Developer's servers and is stored only on the user's device.

### d. Information the Developer does not collect

- Advertising identifiers (IDFA), location data, contacts, photos, device identifiers, and payment information
- The Developer does not track users and does not include any third-party advertising or analytics software development kits (SDKs) in the Service.

## 3. Retention and Use Period

1. The Developer retains personal information until the member withdraws, and destroys it without delay upon a withdrawal request.
2. Notwithstanding paragraph 1, information the Developer is required to retain under applicable laws is kept for the period prescribed by those laws and then destroyed.

## 4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Developer does not provide users' personal information to third parties, except in the following cases:

1. Where the user has given prior consent
2. Where required by law, or where an investigative agency makes a request in accordance with the procedures and methods prescribed by law for investigative purposes

## 7. Destruction of Personal Information

1. The Developer destroys personal information without delay once the retention period has elapsed or the processing purpose has been achieved and the information is no longer necessary.
2. Personal information stored in electronic file format is permanently deleted using technical methods that make recovery or reconstruction impossible.
3. When a member requests withdrawal, account information and budgeting data stored on the server, together with data stored on the user's device, are deleted. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

## 8. Rights of Users and How to Exercise Them

1. You may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information, or withdraw your consent.
2. You can view, edit, and delete budgeting data directly through the app, and delete your account information through the account deletion feature in the app's [Settings] screen.
3. Other requests may be sent to the email address of the Privacy Officer below, and the Developer will act on them without delay.
4. The Developer handles such requests within the scope prescribed by law and, where there is a legitimate reason not to act, informs the user of that reason.

## 9. Security Measures

1. All communication between the user's device and the Developer's servers is transmitted over an encrypted protocol (HTTPS/TLS).
2. The number of personnel with access to personal information is kept to a minimum.
3. Budgeting data stored on the server is segregated by member identifier so that no member can access another member's data.
4. Data stored on the user's device is subject to the file protection features provided by iOS.

## 10. Automatic Collection Devices

The Developer does not install or operate any device — such as cookies or advertising identifiers — that automatically collects behavioral information about users.
